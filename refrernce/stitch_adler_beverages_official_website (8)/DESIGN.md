## Brand & Style

This design system defines a premium, modern, authentic Indian beverage brand with global aspirations. Rooted in the poise of focused vitality—encapsulated by the ethos "FOCUS LIKE AN EAGLE" and the promise "Where Health Meets Refreshment"—the identity marries regal heritage with contemporary functional wellness.

The visual style blends **Minimalism** with **Tactile Luxury**. Clean, expansive layouts provide modern breathing room, while deep royal pigments, warm metallic gold detailing, and crisp contrast convey prestige and craftsmanship. Interfaces evoke an aura of executive clarity, natural potency, and artisanal care without feeling ornamental or cluttered.

## Colors

The palette balances intense regal authority with grounding earthy warmth and targeted natural vibrance:

- **Primary (`#2B0D47` / Rich Deep Purple):** Anchors hero sections, top-tier packaging badges, primary actions, and brand header blocks. It communicates regal authenticity and concentrated potency.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059` / Warm Metallic Gold):** Used deliberately for luxury trims, rating badges, key call-to-action highlights, premium crests, and borders.
- **Neutral (`#231610` / Deep Espresso Brown & `#FAF8F5` Warm Off-White):** Deep espresso serves as the primary text color and high-contrast dark foundation, imparting more organic warmth than harsh black. The canvas background defaults to `#FAF8F5` for an editorial, tactile feel, accented with pure `#FFFFFF` for elevated card surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#4A8B3C` / Fresh Botanical Green):** Reserved strictly for micro-accents, functional wellness tags, Ayurvedic ingredient markers, and botanical badges. Never used for full-bleed background fills.

## Typography

The typography pairing contrasts **Syne**—an avant-garde, structural display face—with **Plus Jakarta Sans**—a crisp, legible, and contemporary geometric sans.

- **Syne** is reserved for headlines, statements, and key brand numbers. Its distinct geometric character establishes immediate global luxury and bold executive focus.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers optimal legibility for nutritional breakdowns, ingredient transparency, narrative storytelling, and UI controls.
- Letter spacing is expanded on labels (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to mimic high-end fragrance and luxury retail labeling.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt base grid within a 12-column responsive layout for desktop/tablet, collapsing to a single-column or dual-column stacked flow on mobile screens.

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Generous 3rem canvas margins with 1.5rem gutters provide an editorial, expansive luxury layout where hero bottles, ingredients, and typography have room to breathe.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Standardized 2rem canvas margins with 1.25rem gutters.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Compact 1.25rem canvas margins and 1rem gutters, ensuring clean touch targets and clear information hierarchy.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal layering and warm ambient shadows** rather than harsh, cold drop shadows.

- **Base Surfaces:** Warm Off-White (`#FAF8F5`) for canvas; Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for raised cards.
- **Ambient Shadow (Resting):** `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(35, 22, 16, 0.06)` — tinted with Espresso Brown to maintain organic richness.
- **Ambient Shadow (Hover/Elevated):** `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(43, 13, 71, 0.12)` — tinted subtly with Rich Purple to give active components a luminous, royal lift.
- **Luxury Outlines:** Subtle `1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)` gold borders are preferred over flat gray lines to separate product cards and modular feature grids.

## Shapes

The design system employs refined, architectural roundedness:
- Standard elements (inputs, standard buttons, notification cards) use `0.5rem` (rounded).
- Elevated product containers and feature banners use `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) up to `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`).
- Micro tags, dietary badges, and pill indicators utilize fully curved pill shapes (`9999px`) to create an inviting contrast against bold rectangular grid blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Solid Rich Purple (`#2B0D47`) fill, Crisp White label in Syne/Plus Jakarta Sans bold, rounded to `0.5rem` or pill-shaped for purchase actions. Hover state illuminates with a soft gold border glow (`#C5A059`).
- **Secondary Luxury Button:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in Warm Metallic Gold (`#C5A059`), deep purple or espresso text. Hover fills with gold at 10% opacity.
- **Tertiary/Ghost:** Plain text in `#2B0D47` with an underline transition on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Botanical / Health Badge:** Very pale botanical tint background (`rgba(74, 139, 60, 0.12)`) paired with Fresh Botanical Green (`#4A8B3C`) text and an optional micro leaf/origin icon.
- **Heritage / Premium Badge:** Warm metallic gold border with deep purple text and subtle gold sheen.

### Product Cards
- Built on Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) containers over the Off-White canvas.
- Features high-contrast beverage imagery, subtle `1px` gold trim, Syne display typography for flavor profiles, and bold primary action buttons.
- Soft brown-tinted resting shadow (`rgba(35, 22, 16, 0.06)`), elevating smoothly with a purple ambient glow on hover.

### Input Fields
- Background: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- Border: `1px solid rgba(35, 22, 16, 0.2)`. Focus state transforms border into `1.5px` Rich Purple (`#2B0D47`) with a faint Gold ambient halo.
- Labels: `label-md` uppercase, tracked out (`0.06em`) in Espresso Brown.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular and softly rounded squares matching `0.25rem` radius.
- Active states filled with Rich Purple (`#2B0D47`), displaying a crisp white or gold checkmark.
